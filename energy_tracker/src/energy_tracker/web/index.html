    System cost and settings
    Savings are what your household's electricity would have cost with no solar or battery, less what you actually paid. By default the comparison uses your own tariff, so cheap-rate charging of a car still counts as cheap. To measure against a different tariff, add it under Compare tariffs and choose it here. The three yearly percentages are optional: fill any in to see a second break-even date that allows for them. Leave them empty to keep to today's prices and performance.

    Bill check

    ImportedCharged ExportedExport payment
    Add a bill
    Enter the figures from a bill or statement to compare them with what the tracker measured over the same dates. Fill in whichever you have, or choose the bill's PDF and press Read to have them filled in for you to check. Choose several PDFs at once to read them all into a list and save them together. PDFs are read on this device and are not kept or sent anywhere. "Charged" is the electricity charge including the standing charge and VAT, before any export payment. The inverter and the supplier's meter are separate instruments, so a difference of a percent or two is normal.

    Add a tariff to compare
    Enter another tariff's prices to see what your actual usage would have cost on it. To test a price change on your own tariff, choose "Copy my current rates" and alter the prices. Enter prices before VAT with the VAT percentage, or prices including VAT with VAT set to 0.

    Tariff rates

    FromTariffImportExport Standing charge
    Add or change rates
    For a price change, enter the date the new rates start: earlier days keep their old rates. To correct rates already listed, choose Edit on that row. Enter prices as they appear on your bill before VAT and set VAT to 5, or enter prices that already include VAT and set VAT to 0. VAT is added to import and the standing charge, not to export. The standard rate applies all day unless you add time windows with a different rate: one for a single cheap period, or several. For a tariff whose price changes every half hour (Octopus Agile), choose it under half-hourly prices: each half hour is then charged at the price published for it, which already includes VAT, and the rates typed here are only used where no price has been published.
