System cost and settings
Savings are what your household's electricity would have cost with no solar or battery, less what you actually paid. By default the comparison uses your own tariff, so cheap-rate charging of a car still counts as cheap. To measure against a different tariff, add it under Compare tariffs and choose it here. The three yearly percentages are optional: fill any in to see a second break-even date that allows for them. Leave them empty to keep to today's prices and performance.

For money earned on top of your tariff, such as Axle Energy payments for exporting during grid events. It counts towards Payback. Add entries by hand here. If Home Assistant has an Axle event sensor, export events are also recorded automatically, with the payment estimated as the energy exported during the event times the rate below. Use Edit on an entry to replace an estimate with what you were actually paid.

Enter another tariff's prices to see what your actual usage would have cost on it. To test a price change on your own tariff, choose "Copy my current rates" and alter the prices. Enter prices before VAT with the VAT percentage, or prices including VAT with VAT set to 0.

Add or change rates
For a price change, enter the date the new rates start: earlier days keep their old rates. To correct rates already listed, choose Edit on that row. Enter prices as they appear on your bill before VAT and set VAT to 5, or enter prices that already include VAT and set VAT to 0. VAT is added to import and the standing charge, not to export.
