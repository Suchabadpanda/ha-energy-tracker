Cost by device
Each device pays for the electricity that actually supplied it, half hour by half hour: grid power at that half hour's price, and battery power at what charging the battery cost. So a car charged overnight costs the night rate, and the house run from the battery by day carries the cost of charging it. Battery export is electricity stored in the battery and later sold back to the grid: what it cost to store is no device's, and the payment for it is in Export credit. The standing charge and export credit belong to the house as a whole and are left out. See "Running costs by device" on the Devices page for earlier months. The heat pump's heating and hot water are measured from its own modes (the hot_water_mode_entity option) or else the hot water tank's reheats (hot_water_tank_entity); on other days, up to the heat pump's usual warm-day use counts as hot water and the rest as heating. Its COP (today) and SCOP (the month) are estimates: see Estimated SCOP on the Devices page.
